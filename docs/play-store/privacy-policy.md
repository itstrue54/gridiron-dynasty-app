# Privacy policy

_Last updated: September 2026_

This app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for.
- **No network access.** The app does not request permission to use the
  internet and makes no network requests.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking.** None are included.
- **Your saves stay on your device.** Your league is saved in the app's own
  private storage and is removed if you uninstall the app.
- **Exports are yours.** If you export a roster, the app writes a spreadsheet
  file to your device's Downloads folder. It goes nowhere else.

The teams and players in the game are fictional and generated by the app.

If you have a question about this policy, open an issue at
https://github.com/itstrue54/nflsimtext.
