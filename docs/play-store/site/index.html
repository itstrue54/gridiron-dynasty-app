Privacy policy

Gridiron Dynasty. Last updated: October 2026.

This app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No network access. The app does not request permission to use the internet and makes no network requests.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. None are included.
  • Your saves stay on your device. Your league is saved in the app's own private storage and is removed if you uninstall the app.
  • Files you choose stay on your device. If you start a league from your own roster file, the app reads the file you pick and nothing else.
  • Exports are yours. If you export a roster, a roster template or a season's standings, the app writes the file to your device's Downloads folder. It goes nowhere else.
  • Sharing is up to you. If you share a season summary, the app hands it to your phone's share sheet, and it goes only where you send it.

The teams and players in the game are fictional and generated by the app.
