Coaching staffs: what they did to development
=============================================

Seed 2026, 10 seasons, `dynasty --years=10`. NET DEV is mean rating points
gained per player per offseason - first year to last.

  POINT                          NET DEV        WHAT CHANGED
  original (pre-reorder)         0.40 -> 0.02   docs/baseline-dynasty.txt
  after phase reorder            0.61 -> 0.32   development moved to SPEC phase 11
  coaching, N(50,15) ratings     0.59 -> 0.29   real staffs, honest rating spread
  coaching, N(65,12) ratings     0.61 -> 0.23   recentred on the old league mean

Read those four rows carefully, because the obvious reading is wrong.

The phase reorder is what fixed development. Coaching did not improve it and
was never going to: it replaced a stand-in formula (55 + teamId % 25, range
55-79, mean ~67) with generated coaches, and the first attempt generated them
at N(50,15). That dropped league-average coaching by seventeen points and took
progression down with it. Recentring to N(65,12) clamped 35-95 puts the mean
back where SPEC 7.1 was calibrated and restores year one exactly (0.61).

The last-year figure falling to 0.23 is not a regression in development. It is
the age mix moving: this league now carries 459 players aged 21-24 against the
original baseline's 534, and 423 aged 28-30 against 385. Per bracket,
development is slightly *up* on the original baseline -

  BRACKET   ORIGINAL   NOW
  21-24       1.34     1.41
  25-27       0.51     0.63
  28-30      -1.18    -1.21
  31+        -3.03    -3.14

- so the aggregate falls because there are more old players in it, not because
anyone develops worse. NET DEV alone cannot tell those apart, which is why the
bracket split is the number to watch.

What coaching is actually worth
-------------------------------

Best eight staffs develop their players 0.09 rating points a year faster than
the worst eight, correlation 0.33.

Both of those numbers are the wrong measurement, and the right one is worse.
SPEC 7.1 applies the coach multiplier only where `ageFactor > 0` - coaching is
absent from the decline branch entirely. So a whole-roster average measures a
team's age profile far more than it measures its staff, and an old roster reads
as a badly coached one. Restricted to the players coaching can actually reach,
the under-25s, the gap is 0.03 a year at correlation 0.09: indistinguishable
from nothing. (That is the five-seed mean from the table below. The single run
appended at the end reads 0.08 on seed 2026 alone, which is the same number
plus noise - one seed cannot resolve a gap this small, which is rather the
point.)

Three compressions stack up, and the ratings are the smallest of them:

1. SPEC 7.1 caps it. `coachMult = 0.85 + 0.30 * (coaching/100) * (coachability/100)`
   gives coaching at most 0.30 of the multiplier. Across the observed staff
   range of 52 to 75 that is a 3.7% swing in development rate.

2. Coachability halves it again. It multiplies the coaching term, so the
   average player at coachability 50 receives half of whatever range remains.

3. Team staff rating barely varies. Coaches are N(65,12), but a team's
   effective rating is one head coach blended with ten position coaches, and
   averaging ten draws collapses the spread to about sd 5 - an observed league
   range of 52 to 75, not the 35-95 the generator can produce.

What it would take
------------------

Five seeds per config, ten seasons each. The league-average multiplier is held
constant throughout (base = 0.9475 - 0.325k), so these change only the spread
between staffs, never how much the league develops overall. U25 GAP is rating
points per year between the top eight staffs and the bottom eight, on players
24 and under.

  CONFIG                  K     SD  STAFF RANGE  U25 GAP  CORREL
  current              0.30     12       53-74      0.03    0.09
  slope x2             0.60     12       53-74      0.00    0.02
  slope x3.3           1.00     12       53-74      0.07    0.16
  wider ratings only   0.30     20       46-78      0.02   -0.00
  slope x3.3 + wider   1.00     20       46-78      0.11    0.26
  slope x6.7 + wider   2.00     20       46-78      0.26    0.53

Measurement noise on the gap is about +/-0.04, so everything above the last two
rows is zero. Read three things off this table:

- Widening the ratings alone does nothing (0.02, correlation -0.00). The
  generator is not the problem, and re-rolling coach ratings will not fix it.
- The slope is the lever, but it has to move a long way. Only at k = 2.00 does
  staff quality explain a meaningful share of young-player development
  (correlation 0.53, about 28% of variance).
- Holding the mean fixed, k cannot exceed ~2.9 before the base term goes
  negative and bad coaching starts actively shrinking players. k = 2.00 is
  close to the structural ceiling of this formula, not a comfortable setting
  inside it.

The formula is [LOCKED], so this is a spec question. Worth noting that SPEC 7.1
states its own intent as "coaching quality matters most for coachable young
players - which makes the position-coach hiring screen meaningful." The formula
as written does not deliver that; the change would bring it in line with what
the section already says it is for.
